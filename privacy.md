# Ija Belote - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06

Ija Belote (package `com.ijagames.ijabelote`) is a Tunisian Belote card game for Android. This policy explains what the app stores, what it shares and how you can ask us to delete your data.

Developer: Sellami Ahmed. Contact: **ahmed.sellami@zohomail.com**

## What the app stores on your device
- Your display name, avatar letter, sound and vibration settings and game preferences.
- A random identifier that the app creates the first time you use online play. It identifies your guest account on our server. It is not your advertising ID and is not linked to your phone number or contacts.

## Online play (our server)
When you play online, our game server stores:
- your account identifier, display name and avatar;
- if you choose to link an e-mail address: the e-mail address and your password (stored only as a salted hash, never in clear);
- your game statistics and a short history of your recent games;
- the display name and seat you take at a table, which other players at that table and in the public table list can see.

Chat messages are passed to the players at your table and are not kept for any other use. The server is reached over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection through a relay service (Tailscale Funnel) that carries encrypted traffic only.

We use this data only to run the game, keep your progress and statistics, prevent abuse and fix problems. We do not sell it.

## Ads
The app shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**: a banner on the menu screens and a full-screen ad after a finished game. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use your device's **advertising ID**, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data. How Google uses this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

- **Consent:** in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (and wherever the law requires it) the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested.
- **Your control on Android:** you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your phone's settings (Settings, Google, Ads).
- **Children:** the game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## What we do not do
- We do not sell your personal data.
- We do not access your contacts, photos, files, location or microphone.
- We do not share your data with anyone except as described here: Google (ads) and the infrastructure used to run the server.

## Permissions
- **Internet:** to connect to the game server and to load ads.
- **Vibrate:** for haptic feedback (can be turned off in settings).
- **Advertising ID (`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`):** used by the ads described above.

## Retention and deleting your data
Your online data is kept while your account exists. To delete your account and the data tied to it (profile, e-mail, statistics, history), send an e-mail to **ahmed.sellami@zohomail.com** from the address linked to the account, or, if you only used a guest account, with your player name and the date you last played. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by e-mail. Data stored only on your phone is removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

## Security
Connections to the server are encrypted and passwords are hashed. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use elsewhere.

## Changes
When this policy changes we update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
