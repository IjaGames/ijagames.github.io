# Ija Belote - Delete your account and data

Last updated: 2026-11-06

This page explains how to delete your Ija Belote account (package `com.ijagames.ijabelote`) and the data tied to it. See also the [Privacy Policy](https://ijagames.github.io/privacy.html).

## Option 1: in the app (immediate)

1. Open the app and go to **Profile**, then **Account**.
2. Tap **Delete my account**.
3. Read the warning and tap **Delete permanently**.

This works for guest accounts and for accounts linked to an e-mail. The deletion happens on the server straight away and cannot be undone.

## Option 2: by e-mail

If you cannot open the app, write to **ahmed.sellami@zohomail.com**:

- If you linked an e-mail: write from that address.
- If you only played as a guest: give your player name and the date you last played.

We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

## What is deleted

- Your account, player name and avatar.
- Your e-mail and password hash (if you linked an e-mail).
- Your statistics and your recent games.
- Your leaderboard entry and the reservation of your player name.

## What may remain

- Server backups are kept for at most 14 days and then deleted, so your data also leaves the backups within that time.
- Your player name can still appear in the "recent games" list of players you played with, until that list rolls over (it keeps only the last 20 games).
- Data stored only on your phone is removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.
- Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob under Google's own policy; you can reset your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.
